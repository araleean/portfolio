Launching Hollywood Casino

Role
Senior Product Design Manager
Date
2024–2025
Challenge
Casino gameplay lived inside theScore Bet and ESPN Bet — products built around sports. Casino-first players had to navigate a sportsbook they didn't need, and we couldn't optimize onboarding, lobby hierarchy, or retention mechanics without competing with the sports experience.
Solution
A focused MVP that extracted casino from the sportsbook entirely and launched it under the Hollywood Casino brand — purpose-built for casino-first players, with a new design system, a dedicated retention mechanic, and deep PENN Play loyalty integration.
The Opportunity

Casino had always been a tab. In theScore Bet it launched as a secondary feature, then carried into ESPN Bet — both products designed around the rhythms of sports: live scores, pre-game betting, a content feed tied to the sports calendar. Casino players got a functional but subordinate experience. Promotions, lobby structure, and onboarding were all shaped by sportsbook constraints.

The standalone app changed the equation. Hollywood Casino gave us permission to design exclusively around casino-first behaviour — players motivated by slots, table games, and daily bonuses, not overnight line movements. A dedicated product also meant reaching users who would never download a sportsbook, and building a direct relationship with the Hollywood Casino brand and PENN's loyalty ecosystem.

At MVP, the app launched in New Jersey, Michigan, Pennsylvania, and West Virginia — four regulated real-money casino markets on iOS.

Approach

Casino-first, all the way down

The home screen is the game lobby — no news feed, no live scores, no score alerts. Every navigation and IA decision was made with one question: does this help someone find a game and play it? We removed everything that didn't serve that goal. For a product extracted from a sportsbook, that required active restraint at every design decision.

Building a new brand skin, not porting the old one

Hollywood Casino has its own visual identity — separate from theScore Bet's red-and-black and ESPN Bet's purple. The app uses the Hollywood Casino brand standards: dark backgrounds, gold accents, and the aesthetic associated with PENN's physical properties. This meant building a new design system skin from scratch rather than reskinning the sportsbook UI. Midway through the project, PENN's corporate marketing team finalized an updated brand direction, which reset a significant portion of completed design work. We compressed what's normally a months-long integration into a few weeks, touching approximately 1,600 screens across 11 Figma files.

Optimizing for the first bet

Registration and deposit are the most drop-off-prone moments in any real-money gaming product. We minimized form length, surfaced the welcome offer at the point of first deposit rather than burying it in a promotions hub, and made the game lobby browsable before registration was complete — so users could explore 700+ games before committing to sign up. Every friction point was weighed against a compliance reason for it to exist.

Designing for two audiences in parallel

New casino players need a product that doesn't assume sportsbook fluency — no jargon, clear onboarding, and a low-stakes way to engage before depositing. Experienced players need something that feels complete and credible from day one: a deep game library, fast deposits, and a loyalty programme they recognize. SPIN IT — a daily free-to-play slot mechanic — served both: a no-deposit entry point for new users, and a daily re-engagement hook for active players.

Key Features

Game Lobby

700+ slots and table games at launch, organized by category — Slots, Table Games, Live Casino, Jackpots, New, and Popular. Live dealer surfaces as a distinct category given its higher engagement and longer session times. Recently played and favourites rails reduce navigation friction for returning players. Game thumbnails are standardized in size to enable a consistent grid across providers — which, given the number of external game suppliers involved, required careful spec work with the engineering team.

SPIN IT — Daily Free-to-Play

Three free spins per day, accessible without a deposit. Spins reset at 5pm ET — intentionally timed to the post-work engagement peak, and distinct from the midnight resets most competitors use. Prizes are awarded as Casino Credits to comply with state promotional gaming rules. A weekly chip collection mechanic accumulates across the week toward a larger end-of-week reward pool, encouraging consistent daily returns.

SPIN IT was a deliberate double-duty mechanic: a retention tool for active depositing players, and an acquisition hook for new registrants who hadn't yet put money in. Designing it to work credibly in both contexts — without feeling like a consolation prize to experienced players — was one of the more interesting product design problems on the project.

PENN Play Rewards

Existing PENN Play cardholders can link their card in-app to earn PENN Cash on real-money play and see their tier status — Preferred, Preferred Plus, VIP, Elite. No other online casino app connects to a physical brick-and-mortar loyalty ecosystem at this scale, which made PENN Play a genuine differentiator in the product's positioning. The design challenge was surfacing it contextually — after a session, in the promotions hub — without it cluttering the gameplay experience for players who aren't PENN Play members.

Wallet & Payments

Deposit methods include credit/debit card, PayPal, online banking, and Play+. PayPal was prioritized given its demonstrated impact on conversion in comparable products and its high trust signal for new users. Withdrawal confirmation includes a responsible gaming prompt — both a compliance requirement and an intentional friction point to reduce impulsive withdrawal-and-redeposit behaviour. State-required disclosures are embedded in the confirmation flow without interrupting it.

1x Playthrough

A product-level positioning decision — not just a marketing claim. Most competitors require 10–35x wagering on bonus wins. 1x required alignment across product, legal, and compliance, and became a core differentiator in the welcome offer.

Responsible Gaming

Deposit limits, session time reminders, self-exclusion (temporary and permanent), and reality check pop-ups — all required for licensing in NJ, MI, PA, and WV. Patterns were closely modelled on theScore Bet to minimize legal review cycles.

Geolocation

Verified at session start and re-checked at key transaction points — not continuously, to reduce battery impact. Error messaging includes a clear manual review path for false negatives at state boundaries.

Account Linking

Existing theScore Bet casino users can link their account at first login to restore wallet and game history — a continuity experience for migrated users that required careful state management across two backend systems.

My Role

I directed the design across the iOS app, set quality standards, and was hands-on in key decisions around brand application, component direction, and experience architecture. I also managed the designer responsible for the majority of the execution.

I was the primary design point of contact for the initiative — working across product, engineering, and PENN's external brand team to keep decisions moving and coordinate approvals. That included managing marketing asset delivery timelines, reviewing engineering handoffs, and absorbing the mid-project rebrand without losing momentum.

The project ran on a tight timeline with a hard regulatory launch date. A lot of the role was knowing which decisions could be deferred and which needed to be locked, and keeping alignment across a large, cross-functional team working under real pressure.
