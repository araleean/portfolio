Refer a Friend

Referral programs are a proven growth lever — but in sports betting, you can't just copy what works in consumer apps.

Role
Senior Product Design Manager
Platform
iOS, Android
Challenge
theScore Bet had no referral program. Building one meant navigating tight regulatory constraints that govern promotions in gambling — varying by state and province.
Solution
A referral flow built from scratch — native to the app, rewarding to users, and compliant across all markets. Validated with user testing before committing to build.
The Opportunity

theScore Bet didn't have a referral program. From a business and growth perspective, it was an obvious gap — referrals are one of the highest-converting acquisition channels, and sports betting is inherently social. Fans watch together, talk about bets, and share tips.

The challenge was building something that felt native to the app and rewarding to users, while staying within the tight regulatory constraints that govern promotions in gambling — which vary by state and province.

How I Set It Up

I worked with the designer to structure a process that got the right people aligned before any concepts were explored. We ran cross-functional workshops with product, legal, marketing, and engineering to map the full referral journey — from the moment a user decides to share, to when their friend makes their first deposit.

We then ran user testing on early concepts to validate the mechanic before committing to build. In a regulated space, getting the framing and language right matters as much as the design.

The Design

A referral flow that felt rewarding without feeling predatory — clear on the offer, transparent on the terms, and easy to share.
