theScore Bet & ESPN Bet

Overview

Challenge

theScore Bet had no referral program. Building one meant navigating tight regulatory constraints that govern promotions in gambling — varying by state and province.

Solution

A referral flow built from scratch — native to the app, rewarding to users, and compliant across all markets. Validated with user testing before committing to build.

Referrals are one of the highest-converting acquisition channels, and sports betting is inherently social — fans watch together, talk about bets, and share tips. The challenge was building something that felt native to the app and rewarding to users while staying compliant.

Research

We ran cross-functional workshops with product, legal, marketing, and engineering to map the full referral journey — from the moment a user decides to share, to when their friend makes their first deposit. We then ran user testing on early concepts to validate the mechanic before committing to build.

Research artifacts — workshops, user testing
Design

A referral flow that felt rewarding without feeling predatory — clear on the offer, transparent on the terms, and easy to share.
