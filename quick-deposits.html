The Problem

On theScore Bet, depositing required users to navigate multiple steps away from wherever they were in the app. The business saw an opportunity to surface it closer to the moment of intent — but wanted to move fast and skip research.

I pushed back. In a payments flow, a poor experience doesn't just cause drop-off — it erodes trust. We ran a lightweight usability test before shipping, ran cross-functional workshops to align the team, and phased the rollout starting with Trustly users only.

Phase 1 — Trustly

Quick Deposit surfaced from anywhere in the app for users with Trustly as their saved payment method.
