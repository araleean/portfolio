Quick Deposits

Surfacing deposits closer to the moment of intent — a feature that captured 12% of all app deposits within weeks of launch.

Overview

Challenge

Depositing required users to navigate multiple steps away from wherever they were in the app. The business wanted to move fast and skip research — I pushed back.

Solution

A surfaced deposit entry point accessible from anywhere in the app, validated with user testing before launch and phased starting with Trustly users.

In a payments flow, a poor experience doesn't just cause drop-off — it erodes trust. We ran a lightweight usability test before shipping, ran cross-functional workshops to align the team, and phased the rollout starting with Trustly users only.
