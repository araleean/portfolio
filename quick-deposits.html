Quick Deposits

Surfacing deposits closer to the moment of intent — a feature that captured 12% of all app deposits within weeks of launch.

Role
Senior Product Design Manager
Timeline
~1 month
Challenge
Depositing required users to navigate multiple steps away from wherever they were in the app. The business wanted to move fast and skip research — I pushed back.
Solution
A surfaced deposit entry point accessible from anywhere in the app, validated with user testing before launch and phased starting with Trustly users.
The Problem

On theScore Bet, depositing required users to navigate multiple steps away from wherever they were in the app. The business saw an opportunity to surface it closer to the moment of intent — but wanted to move fast and skip research.

I pushed back. In a payments flow, a poor experience doesn't just cause drop-off — it erodes trust. We ran a lightweight usability test before shipping, ran cross-functional workshops to align the team, and phased the rollout starting with Trustly users only.

Phase 1 — Trustly

Quick Deposit surfaced from anywhere in the app for users with Trustly as their saved payment method.

Phase 1 launched end of September 2024. Early results validated both the design and the decision to test before shipping.
