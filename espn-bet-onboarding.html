When Barstool Sportsbook became ESPN Bet, we used the rebrand as an opportunity to rethink onboarding from the ground up — across all active US states.

Role Senior Product Design Manager
Timeline 2 quarters
Platform iOS, Android, Web
The Problem

The existing sign-up flow had too many steps and too much drop-off. Rather than treating the rebrand as a reskin, we used it as a chance to fix what we knew wasn't working — with research, cross-functional alignment, and a design system built to scale across brands and regions.

Sign-Up Flow

Simplified, state-adaptive onboarding with smarter KYC sequencing — ID upload moved to withdrawal, email verification deferred post sign-up.
