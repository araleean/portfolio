ESPN Bet

Redesigning
Sign-Up

The sign-up flow had friction points that were costing us users. We had an opportunity to fix them.

Role Senior Product Design Manager
Team Product Designer, Product Manager, User Researcher, Design Systems, Engineers
Timeline 3 months
Platform iOS, Android
The Problem

The ESPN Bet sign-up flow was lengthy — too many steps, too much friction, and measurable drop-off as a result. Users were abandoning the flow before completing registration, which meant lost players before they ever placed a bet.

There was a clear opportunity to streamline the experience without cutting corners on compliance. In a regulated space, every step exists for a reason — the challenge was finding where we could reduce friction without compromising what we were legally required to collect.

Sign-Up Flow

Simplified, state-adaptive onboarding with smarter KYC sequencing — ID upload moved to withdrawal, email verification deferred post sign-up.

Outcome

Launched as an MVP in Arizona in April 2025, with a full rollout across all states and brands planned for Q3 2025 — timed for football season.

Lift in sign-up completion — reduced friction led to more users completing onboarding
