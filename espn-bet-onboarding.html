Overview

Challenge

The sign-up flow was lengthy, had too many steps and created a lot of friction — leading to users abandoning before completing registration.

Solution

We optimized the experience without cutting corners on compliance and worked with research to dig into the user frustrations.

Research

We ran user testing on the existing flow to identify where users were dropping off and what was causing the most friction.

Design

We explored multiple directions before aligning on an approach — reducing steps, deferring non-critical fields, and making compliance requirements feel less intimidating.

Final Design

The redesigned flow reduced the number of steps, surfaced progress clearly, and deferred optional fields to post-registration.
