Launching Hollywood Casino

Taking casino from embedded feature to standalone product — across iOS, Android, and web.

Role
Senior Manager, Product Design
Brand
Hollywood Casino
Year
2024
Challenge

The casino experience lived inside theScore Bet and ESPN Bet — bundled with a sportsbook that casino-only users didn't need. We had to extract it, rebrand it, and ship it as a standalone product on a tight timeline.

Solution

A focused MVP that uncoupled casino from sportsbook entirely, paired with a new brand identity — launched in Pennsylvania, then expanded to Michigan, New Jersey, West Virginia, and Ontario.

The Opportunity

Casino had always lived alongside sports betting in our apps — but for users who only wanted to play slots or table games, navigating a full sportsbook to get there added unnecessary friction.

Hollywood Casino gave us the chance to build something purpose-built for casino players: a cleaner entry point, a dedicated experience, and a brand with its own identity. My role spanned directing the design work, contributing to key decisions hands-on, and managing the designer responsible for the bulk of the execution.

Approach

Starting from what we had

Rather than building from scratch, we stripped the casino experience out of the existing ESPN Bet and theScore Bet apps — removing sportsbook content, wagers, promotions, and notifications that wouldn't apply to casino-only users.

Designing for two audiences

We thought carefully about novice and experienced casino players. For first-timers, a standalone casino app removes a lot of the jargon and complexity that comes with sports betting. For experienced players, the experience needed to feel complete and credible from day one.

Navigating the rebrand

Midway through, the corporate marketing team finalized a new Hollywood Casino brand — which reset a significant portion of the design work. We worked closely with the design systems team to integrate the new color tokens and visual identity under a tight deadline, compressing what's usually a months-long process into a few weeks.

The Outcome

Launched in 5 markets

Pennsylvania first, followed by Michigan, New Jersey, West Virginia, and Ontario (as theScore Casino).

Two rebrands, one launch
