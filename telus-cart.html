Building an E-Commerce Cart

Role
Product Designer
Brand
TELUS
Year
2017
Overview

Challenge

The previous experience took users from a product page directly into a "buy now" flow. Users couldn't add more than one item or save purchases for another day.

Solution

A phased cart experience — launching with accessories, then expanding to support phones and plans — built to scale across TELUS's full product ecosystem.

To validate, learn and improve the cart experience, we launched in three phases: accessories only, then phones and new activations, then all mobility users.

Research

During discovery, we captured all the Jobs to be Done for both the consumer and business. Once we prioritized the needs, we brainstormed features that would help accomplish them. We then began wireframing a few options and user tested to get validation.

Phase 1: Accessories Cart

Product Detail Page

From the product detail page, users can select their colour, size and quantity. To add the item to cart, users can simply click on the "Add to cart" button.

Dropdown Notification

After the item has been added to cart, a notification will dropdown by the cart icon to confirm the action. The button will also change to "Added to cart" for 3 seconds.

Accessories Cart Review

The cart review page was created to allow users to edit and confirm their order prior to checkout.

Phase 2: Phones & Plans Cart

Product Experience Page

We discovered that users wanted more education and information about devices to help decide which phone is best for them.

Product Configure Page

Once users click on one of the phones, they're directed to configure their phone and choose the right plan for them.

Cart Review

After configuring their phone/plan, they're directed to the cart review page. Due to technical limitations, we were unable to unify the phones and accessories flows together.
